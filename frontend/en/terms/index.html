Last updated: November 2026.

Current status: beta

Toojibee is currently in early (beta) development. By using the web version or installing the Android build, you acknowledge it may contain bugs, that data may be reset or lost in future updates, and that features may change without notice.

Account

Signing up needs an email, a username, your gender, date of birth and a password, and you must be at least 9 years old. Gender and date of birth can't be changed after sign-up. You're responsible for keeping your password safe; email confirmation and a password reset link help protect it. You can change your password or delete your account from your profile at any time.

Your content

Tasks, habits, events and any other data you enter into Toojibee belong to you. They're stored on Toojibee's own server (hosted in Iran) so you can reach them from different devices — see the Privacy Policy for full details.

Third-party services

Toojibee uses no third-party services (no analytics, ads, or syncing with other calendars); the site, the app and the database all run on Toojibee's own server. If an integration (such as Google sign-in) is added later, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated before it's turned on.

Changes to the service

Because Toojibee is still in beta, features, design, and even the scope of what's included may change between releases.

Limitation of liability

Toojibee is provided "as is", with no guarantee of stability or availability. Until a stable release, there's no SLA or uptime guarantee.

Account deletion

There's no in-app account deletion option yet; to request deletion of your account and data from the server, contact us at the email below.
