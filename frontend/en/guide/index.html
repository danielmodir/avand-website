User guide

A step-by-step look at planning your days with Toojibee.

1. Get started

"Get started" opens the web version. You can browse the calendar and occasions without an account; to create tasks, habits or events, sign up so your data is saved and available on your other devices.

2. Calendar

The calendar shows your days, dates and occasions. Switch between the daily, weekly and monthly views, and tap a day to see its plan.

3. Tasks

Use the + button to create a task, pick its day (or a range of days), and tick it off when it's done. Tasks can have subtasks. An unfinished task doesn't move to the next day on its own — its status is kept and you decide what to do with it.

4. Habits

Add the habits you want to repeat and choose the days they repeat on. Each day, a single tick records it, so you can follow your progress.

5. Events

Create an event for appointments and other timed plans; it appears on the calendar next to your tasks and habits.

6. Reminders

Reminders for tasks and events are coming soon; for now, the calendar and the planning desk show what each day holds.

7. Day and night mode

The sun/moon button at the top switches between day and night mode.
