About Toojibee

What is Toojibee?

Toojibee is a time and habit management app — where your tasks, habits, events, and calendar live together in one experience, instead of several apps you have to keep switching between.

Why was it built?

Most productivity apps focus on either a to-do list or habit tracking, not both. When your daily planning is spread across several apps, it gets hard to see "what do I actually have today." Toojibee starts from the assumption that tasks, habits, and events belong on one shared calendar, not scattered across separate tools.

What problem does it solve?

Instead of switching between apps, Toojibee gives you a daily/weekly/monthly view that shows unfinished tasks, daily habits, and time-bound events together — with one simple rule: an unfinished task doesn't automatically roll over to the next day, it just keeps its "not done" status.

Core features

The current build (MVP) covers task, habit, and event management plus a daily/weekly/monthly calendar with customizable reminders. Features like an AI assistant, a tool store, and a connections network are still being designed, and aren't advertised publicly until they're actually ready.

Current product status

Toojibee is currently an early (beta) build. The web version available from this site works with real sign-up, and your data (tasks, habits, events, profile) is actually stored on a server — see the Privacy Policy for full details. Some features may change in future releases.
