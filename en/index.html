Why Avand?

Avand's goal is to bring tasks, habits, events, a calendar, and (eventually) AI together under one roof — not to be just another calendar app.

  • Everything on one shared calendar

    Instead of switching between a separate to-do app and a separate habit tracker, they all show up together on one calendar.

  • An unfinished task doesn't get lost

    It doesn't automatically roll over to the next day — it just keeps its "not done" status, and you decide what to do next.

  • Built with AI in mind

    A place for a smart assistant is already part of the design — for when it's actually ready.
