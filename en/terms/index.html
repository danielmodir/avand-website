When you sign up with email/password, a real account is created on our server. You're responsible for keeping your login details confidential. Email verification and password recovery aren't implemented yet — these will be added before a stable release.

Your content

Tasks, habits, events, and any other data you enter into Toojibee belong to you. This data is stored on our server (Supabase infrastructure) so you can access it from different devices — see the Privacy Policy for full details.

The current build of Toojibee uses Supabase infrastructure (database and authentication); other than that, no third-party service (analytics, ads, syncing with other calendars) is enabled.
