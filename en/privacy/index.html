Toojibee Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This reflects the product's actual current state, not a finalized legal document for a complete product.

This website

Toojibee's marketing site (the page you're reading) has no forms, no tracking cookies, and no third-party analytics, and it doesn't collect any personal information from visitors. The only thing this site might ever receive from you is the app installer (APK) you knowingly choose to download.

The web preview

The web version opened from the "Try web preview" button is an early but real, working version: when you sign up or sign in, a real account is created, and your tasks, habits, events, profile info, and connections are actually stored and kept on a server — not just in your browser's memory. That means signing in from another device shows you the same data.

What's stored, and where

Your account email, password (hashed, never stored in plain text), profile info (name, username, phone, gender and date of birth if you enter them), calendar tasks/habits/events, and your list of connections with other users. This data is kept on Supabase's infrastructure — a cloud service whose servers are located outside Iran. If a third-party integration (like Google sign-in or Google Calendar sync) is added later, this section will state exactly what data is shared with that service.

Since this is still a beta release, we haven't yet implemented email verification or a formal security review for this part. The connection between your browser and the server uses HTTPS.

Account and data deletion

There's no in-app "delete account" option yet. To request full deletion of your account and data from the server, contact us at the email below for now; this will become a direct in-app option before the stable release.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS. No tracking code or third-party script is loaded.
